Do I need a drum or practice pad?+

You can practice by tapping the screen. If you have a pad, place your phone nearby and use microphone input. Rhythm:On supports iOS 17 or later and Android 10 or later.

Will it pick up my hits in a noisy room?+

Microphone detection depends on room noise, pad sound, distance and your device. Set up in a quiet spot and recalibrate in Settings when your surroundings change. Timing calibration is available for Bluetooth earphones. The app evaluates hit timing, not posture or hand technique.

What happens to my microphone audio and practice data?+

Raw microphone audio is analysed only on your device and is never recorded, stored or sent. Detailed practice records stay on your device. Online rankings store data such as your anonymous ID, dice name, challenge level, score, timestamps and reward records on the server. Use ‘Delete league record’ in Settings to remove server records. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

How do challenge attempts and weekly rankings work?+

You get one free run daily and one ticket for each different Day cleared, up to three tickets daily. Each Day counts once per day; full Day reviews count, mistake notes do not. Everyone can start at most four runs per day, using Korea time. Every run starts at Lv 1 with the same weekly patterns. Ranking uses cleared level, then score, then who achieved it first, up to the first game over. Continues are personal practice. The week closes Monday at 00:00 Korea time. The website's XP league is a separate ranking.

Is it free? Are there ads or paid options?+

The current App Store version differs from this preview. The new learning and challenge modes are planned for the next update, and the Android release is in preparation. The 41-lesson beginner course is free to access, with limits on failed new lessons and daily challenge attempts. The app includes banner ads and optional rewarded ads; you can change ad preferences in Settings. Check the app for available paid options and prices. Paying does not increase your competitive challenge attempts.
