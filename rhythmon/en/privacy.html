Rhythm:On Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · Provider: DailyDrum

1. Scope

This policy covers the iOS and Android app “Rhythm:On” (the “app”) and this website, provided by DailyDrum. The app never asks for sign-up, email, phone number or your real name. Available features and settings depend on the installed app version.

2. Stored only on your device

Lesson progress and detailed reports, mistake notes, streaks, quests and currency balances, and language, notification and timing settings (including Bluetooth earphone names and their delay) are stored on your device. Challenge records submitted for ranking and reward records are covered by the server section below. On-device records are removed when you delete the app. On-device records may be restored through operating-system backup and restore settings.

3. Microphone

In microphone mode, pad hits are analysed in real time on your device to calculate timing only. Sound is never recorded, saved or sent. You can practice by touch without granting the permission. When developer diagnostics are explicitly enabled, numeric input strength, strike times, detection thresholds and judging results may be kept in the app cache in two files of up to 3 MB each. These contain no raw microphone audio and are not sent automatically. Normal operation and release builds do not create these diagnostic logs.

4. League (stored on our server)

For the weekly league, the following is stored on a server run by DailyDrum (Cloudflare): a random anonymous ID and its credential (stored hashed), your dice name (two word numbers), league tier, weekly XP, lesson/challenge completion events (last 8 days, to prevent duplicates) and the registration time. It is not used to identify you or combined with other data. Players in your group see your name, XP and rank, and this website's league page shows this week's top 20 names, leagues and XP. For the weekly challenge, we store run IDs linked to the anonymous ID, week, receipt date in Korea time, start and receipt times, completed level and score, weekly best, final rank and participant count, reward ID, note amount, claim time, crown status and champion benefit expiry. Per-Day ticket deduplication is handled on your device; Day IDs, raw audio and note-by-note microphone data are not sent. Challenge and reward records have no separate automatic deletion period and are retained until you request deletion of your league record. App participants can see dice names and challenge levels, scores and ranks.

5. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob banner ads and optional rewarded ads. Versions with ad choices check your preference and any required regional consent (UMP) before requesting ads; no ads are requested without a valid consent status. In Settings → Ads & privacy settings, you can change your preference and, where applicable, change or withdraw consent. More restrictive operating-system or regional choices take priority. iOS may request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission only if you choose personalized ads; if you decline, non-personalized ads are requested without IDFA. Non-personalized ads may still process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in device settings. Your ad preference does not change practice features or reward conditions.

6. Paid subscription

If you subscribe, payment is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; DailyDrum never receives card numbers or other payment details. The app only checks whether your subscription is active. Cancel or request refunds in your store account settings.

7. Notifications

Practice reminders are scheduled only on your device, after you grant operating-system permission and enable them in the app; they do not pass through our server. You can turn them off in app settings. Frequency and night-time settings depend on the installed version, and the operating system may delay delivery. We do not collect contacts or phone numbers for reminders.

8. Children

The app does not collect children's personal information. The league uses only an anonymous ID and a dice name, and a parent can delete the league record anytime as described below.

9. Deletion and your rights

Settings → League → “Delete league record” immediately deletes your anonymous ID, name, XP, league and challenge records, and reward records from the server. Local records can be removed by deleting the app or app data, but may be restored from operating-system backups. Consent information may also be stored by the consent-management SDK and operating system; retention and deletion of data processed by advertising services follow Google’s policies. If you cannot delete in the app, for example after losing a device, contact us below.

10. Contact and changes

Contact us through the link below. Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new effective date.
