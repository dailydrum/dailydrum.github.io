Delete your league record (account)

Names and results are linked to an automatically created league account, with no separate sign-up. Request deletion of server-side account, league, challenge and reward records in the app.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Rhythm:On and tap Settings (gear) at the top right.
  2. Open “League”.
  3. Tap “Delete league account and records” and confirm. Earlier versions may call it “Delete league record”.

What gets deleted

Once server deletion is processed, the account ID, credential, dice name, tier, XP, completion events, challenge runs, bests, ranks and rewards are removed. Versions with deletion-pending support wait for server confirmation; reopen the app while connected or retry if needed. You can rejoin with a new league account only after completion. Public web rankings update when their five-minute cache refreshes. Device progress, reports, profile name and notes remain. Cancel subscriptions separately through the store.

If you removed the app or lost the device and cannot delete in the app, contact us below for help identifying the records to delete. Do not post credentials or payment information in a public request.
