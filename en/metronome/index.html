WHY DAILYDRUM TEMPO

Everyone turns on a metronome for a different reason, but we all want the same thing: a steady first beat.

A child picking up an instrument for the first time, an adult who wants to stop rushing, a church keyboardist preparing every week, a drummer heading on stage. DailyDrum has taught timing one-on-one to all of them.

This guide collects the metronome methods we use in lessons, sorted by situation. TEMPO is the metronome we built so you can keep that practice going every day.

  • Practice: subdivisions and accents, tempo trainer, drum machine
  • Prepare: save BPM per song and share setlists
  • Perform: Live screen and voice count-in

TEMPO is a metronome app made by DailyDrum, a drum studio in Anyang (Pyeongchon), South Korea, where every lesson, from children to adults and church musicians, is taught one-on-one.
