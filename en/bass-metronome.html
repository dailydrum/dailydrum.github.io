METRONOME GUIDE

The bass holds the band
together with time.

WHY DAILYDRUM TEMPO

When the bass is half a beat late, the whole band feels heavy.

Bassists make their presence felt through time more than melody. When the kick drum and bass lock together like one body, the song gets solid; when they drift even slightly, everyone feels uncomfortable.

DailyDrum teaches drummers how to lock in with the bass. This time it's the other way around: we put a drum machine in TEMPO so bassists can practice locking in with the drums on their own.

  • Practice bass lines against drum machine kick patterns
  • Build groove with a click on 2 and 4
  • Save BPM per song to get songs into your body before rehearsal

TEMPO is a metronome app made by DailyDrum, a drum studio in Anyang (Pyeongchon), South Korea, where every lesson, from children to adults and church musicians, is taught one-on-one.

Bass metronome practice

  1. Root notes on a quarter-note click · At BPM 70, play root notes as quarter notes. Keep the start and end (length) of each note even.
  2. 8th-note root picking · Turn on the 8th subdivision and keep your picking strength even.
  3. Click on 2 and 4 · Use per-beat mute so only 2 and 4 sound, and hold the groove.
  4. Jam with the drum machine · Play a basic beat and lock your bass to where the kick lands.

Where bassists rush

  • The first note of the bar after a fill
  • Lines with rests (it's easy to count rests short)
  • Slap and 16th-note passages

TEMPO drum machine

  • 4 tracks: kick, snare, hi-hat, ride
  • From basic beats to shuffle, jazz and bossa nova presets
  • 16th swing, tempo control, loop playback
