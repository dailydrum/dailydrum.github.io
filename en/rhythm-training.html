The real causes of bad timing

  • Only "hearing" the beat, not "feeling" it in your body
  • Not counting between beats (subdivisions)
  • Counting rests short
  • Breathing and tempo both speed up when you're nervous

A 4-week rhythm training plan

Week 1 · Count the beat with your body

Turn on the click at BPM 60–70, step on the beat with your foot, and count "one, two, three, four" out loud. Five minutes a day without an instrument is enough.

Week 2 · Split the beat

Turn on the 8th subdivision and clap 8th notes. Once that's comfortable, switch to 16ths and triplets.

Week 3 · Click on 2 and 4

Use per-beat mute so the click sounds only on 2 and 4, and fill in 1 and 3 yourself. You'll develop the backbeat feel of popular music.

Week 4 · Once per bar

Leave the click only on beat one and mute the rest. When you can fill the three empty beats accurately, you can hold the tempo without a click.

FAQ

Can adults fix their timing too?

Yes. Adults understand quickly, so with the right method they can improve noticeably in a short time.
