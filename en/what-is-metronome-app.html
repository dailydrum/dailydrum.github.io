What is a metronome app?

A metronome is a tool that plays the beat at a steady tempo. A metronome app puts that on your phone.

Unlike a wind-up metronome, an app lets you freely change time signatures, subdivisions and accents, save settings per song, and even use a stage screen. That's the biggest difference.

Do these first after installing

  1. Set the BPM · Set the song's tempo as a number. If you don't know it, tap the screen along with the song (TAP) to measure it.
  2. Set the time signature · Most songs are 4/4, waltzes are 3/4, and some ballads are 6/8.
  3. Turn on subdivisions · Splitting the beat into 8ths or 16ths lets you hear wobbles instantly.
  4. Accent beat one · A different sound on beat one keeps you from losing the bar.
  5. Save it as a song · No need to set it up again next time.

What makes a good metronome app

  • A click that stays clear over your instrument
  • Settings you can save per song
  • No ads covering the screen you look at while playing
  • Both practice tools (like tempo increase) and a stage screen

FAQ

Are metronome apps free?

With TEMPO, the metronome, List, Live and one Playback song are free. Ad removal and unlimited Playback songs come with TEMPO PRO, a one-time purchase.

I have a wind-up metronome. Do I need an app too?

If you only practice one piece alone, a wind-up metronome is enough. If you need to remember BPMs for many songs, change meters, subdivisions or accents, or use it in rehearsals and gigs, an app is far more convenient.
