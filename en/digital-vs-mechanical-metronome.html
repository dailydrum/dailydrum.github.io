Before you buy an electronic metronome

Electronic metronome devices have solid buttons and a headphone output. These days, though, a phone and earphones do the same job well. Try practicing with an app for a week first; if something's missing, it's not too late to consider a device.

Advantages unique to digital metronomes

  • Exact BPM numbers and time signature display
  • 8th, 16th and triplet subdivisions and per-beat accents
  • Save your practice settings as songs
  • Practice tools that move the tempo to your target automatically

FAQ

Is a mechanical metronome more accurate?

Purpose matters more than accuracy. A wind-up metronome's beat can change as the spring winds down or if it's tilted, while a digital metronome holds the BPM you set steadily.
