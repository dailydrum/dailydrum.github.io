METRONOME GUIDE

Beyond 4/4,
the world has many meters.

WHY DAILYDRUM TEMPO

Practice a 6/8 song with a 4/4 metronome, and the song's sway disappears.

The rocking of a 6/8 ballad, the one-two-three of a 3/4 waltz, the twisted tension of 7/8: the time signature creates them. But if your metronome only does 4/4, accents land in the wrong places and the character of the song gets lost.

Practice starts with checking the song's time signature. TEMPO, made by DailyDrum, saves the time signature, subdivision and per-beat accents together for every song, so you practice each one with its true character.

  • Time signature and subdivision saved per song
  • Per-beat accent and mute to hear beat groupings
  • Mixed meter to switch some bars of a 4/4 song to 2/4 (coming in 2.5.3)

TEMPO is a metronome app made by DailyDrum, a drum studio in Anyang (Pyeongchon), South Korea, where every lesson, from children to adults and church musicians, is taught one-on-one.

Tips by time signature

MeterFeelPractice tip
2/4March, phrases in twosAccent beat 1, practice quick bar changes
3/4Waltz, one-two-threeOnly the first beat strong, 2 and 3 light
4/4Most popular musicAccent beat 1, backbeat on 2 and 4
6/8Rocking ballad or shuffle feelCount as two groups of three (accent 1 and 4)
7/8Odd meter, irregular tensionDecide the grouping first, like 2+2+3 or 3+2+2

Practicing odd meters

For odd meters like 5/4 and 7/8, the key is dividing the bar into small groups as you count. Accenting the first beat of each group lets you hear where each group starts by sound alone.

Song section voice cues and Playback's option to switch some 4/4 bars to 2/4 are coming in the iOS 2.5.3 update and will be available after App Store review.

For songs whose meter changes mid-song, see the mixed meter metronome guide.
