WHY DAILYDRUM TEMPO

"What BPM is this song?" The answer is always "about this fast."

If you don't know the exact BPM of the song you're practicing, you set the speed by feel every time, and yesterday's practice speed differs from today's. In a band, everyone remembers a different tempo, so you're off from the very first beat.

A song's tempo should be set by number, not by feel. To help anyone keep that habit, DailyDrum, a one-on-one drum studio, put measuring, setting and saving a BPM into one flow on one screen in TEMPO.

  • Measure BPM by tapping with your chosen click sound
  • Save BPM, time signature and subdivision per song
  • TEMPO ANCHOR: re-locks the beat while keeping the sound and downbeat accent

TEMPO is a metronome app made by DailyDrum, a drum studio in Anyang (Pyeongchon), South Korea, where every lesson, from children to adults and church musicians, is taught one-on-one.

How to measure BPM (TAP)

  1. Listen to the original song and tap TAP along with the beat several times
  2. Round off the displayed BPM (e.g. 118.6 → 119 or 120)
  3. Save it in List with the song name

How to use tempo control

  • For practice: start lower, at 60–70% of the original
  • To prepare for the stage: also try 5–10 faster than the original
  • Quick fixes during a live set: change the BPM right on the Live screen

Using it as a click track

When you need a click track for recording or rehearsal, you can load the BPM, meter and count-in saved for each song and start right away. It's a click and count feature; it does not play backing tracks.
