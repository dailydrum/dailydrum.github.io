WHY DAILYDRUM TEMPO

You opened a free metronome, and an ad covered the screen mid-song.

The biggest stress with free apps is ads. A pop-up in the middle of a song, or a banner right next to the controls, breaks your practice flow.

DailyDrum wanted a metronome that even young students could use without worry. So TEMPO keeps its core features free and puts no ads on the playing screens.

  • Metronome, List, Live and one Playback song for free
  • Ads only in designated banner areas of Tempo and List
  • No ads on the Live and Playback screens

TEMPO is a metronome app made by DailyDrum, a drum studio in Anyang (Pyeongchon), South Korea, where every lesson, from children to adults and church musicians, is taught one-on-one.

FAQ

Where can I download a free metronome?

On iPhone and iPad, install TEMPO (DailyDrum) from the App Store. On Android, get it from Google Play.

I want a metronome without ads.

Even in the free version, TEMPO shows no ads on the Live and Playback screens, and a one-time TEMPO PRO purchase removes ads from the whole app.
