WHY DAILYDRUM TEMPO

Your music on the iPad, your beat on the iPhone.

These days the music stand in a practice room holds an iPad instead of paper, and there's always an iPhone in your pocket. There's no reason to carry a separate metronome.

DailyDrum released TEMPO first for iPhone and iPad so students and church musicians could use it on the devices they use most.

  • Free on the App Store, no sign-up
  • Run the stage with Live on the big iPad screen
  • Korean, English, Japanese and Chinese (Simplified/Traditional) supported

TEMPO is a metronome app made by DailyDrum, a drum studio in Anyang (Pyeongchon), South Korea, where every lesson, from children to adults and church musicians, is taught one-on-one.

Start TEMPO free ↗

Why choose TEMPO as your iPhone metronome

  • BPM, time signature, subdivision and count-in saved per song in List
  • Digital · Gentle · Acoustic clicks with per-beat accent and mute
  • Automator and Tracker practice tools
  • Live screen: large controls, voice count-in when you move to the next song
  • TEMPO PRO is a one-time purchase, not a subscription

When the iPad shines

  • See BPM and song order at a glance on a big screen on your music stand
  • When several people share one screen in the rehearsal room
  • Large buttons on the Live screen during a show

How to install

  1. Search for TEMPO (DailyDrum) on the App Store, or tap the button on this page
  2. Open it right after installing (no sign-up)
  3. Set the BPM of your first song and save it in List

The iOS 2.5.3 update (Playback, mixed meter, new click sounds) will be available after App Store review.
